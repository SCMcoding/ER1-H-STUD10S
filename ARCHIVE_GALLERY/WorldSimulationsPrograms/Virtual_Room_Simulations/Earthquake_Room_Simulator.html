Important

Room effects

This version uses layered shading, perspective, smoother quake transitions, reinforced shapes, and physics-based falling so objects wobble, lose support, accelerate under gravity, collide with the floor, and settle.

Hanging lightSwings with a longer period and gradually grows in amplitude.
Books & decorRattle first; individual books then lose support at different times and fall under gravity.
FurnitureThe sofa and table slide; the chair can rock, lose balance, fall under gravity, bounce slightly, and settle.
