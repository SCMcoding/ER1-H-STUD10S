Anatomy note
In an anterior view, the right ventricle forms most of the front surface of the heart. The left ventricle forms the left border and apex. The left atrium lies mostly posteriorly, so only part of it is prominent from the front.

What changes during a beat

The ventricular myocardium shortens and thickens during systole while chamber volume falls. The atria contract late in diastole, just before ventricular systole.

Ventricular fillingTricuspid and mitral valves are open while the aortic and pulmonary valves remain closed.
DiastasisMid-diastolic filling slows as atrial and ventricular pressures approach each other.
Atrial systoleAtria contract late in diastole and complete ventricular filling.
Isovolumetric contractionAll four valves are briefly closed while ventricular pressure rises rapidly.
EjectionAortic and pulmonary valves open once ventricular pressure exceeds pressure in the aorta and pulmonary artery.
