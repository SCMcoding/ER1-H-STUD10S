Temperature note
Color ≠ temperature

The salt changes the light emitted by excited atoms or ions. The actual flame temperature is mainly set by the burner, fuel, air mixture, and heat loss. For that reason, this simulator uses one adjustable burner temperature rather than claiming each salt has its own unique flame temperature.

Laboratory safety
Teacher-supervised demonstration

Real flame tests use open flames and chemical salts. Wear appropriate eye protection, follow the chemical SDS and school/lab procedures, avoid inhaling fumes, and do not perform unknown-chemical flame tests without qualified supervision.
