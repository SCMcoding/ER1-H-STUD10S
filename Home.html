About Me

I'm a graduate of CUNY New York City College of Technology, where I earned my Bachelors in Emerging Media Technology. My academic journey is driven by a passion for research and innovation at the intersection of technology, storytelling, and interactive media.

My work explores how emerging technologies can enhance creative expression and narrative experiences. I’ve developed interactive web projects, crafted immersive game environments, and used AI-driven techniques to create dynamic storytelling elements. Through research, I aim to bridge the gap between traditional media and modern digital tools.

I’m passionate about combining technical expertise with creative problem-solving to push the boundaries of digital storytelling. Welcome to my site—feel free to explore my projects and see how I bring research-driven ideas to life.
